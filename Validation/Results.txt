Delivered behavior
Compact glass capsule expands upward into a four-control dock and inspectors.
Color accent follows the sculpture. Native glass, hover feedback, spring transitions,
and Reduce Motion behavior. Existing sculpture gestures, forms, finishes, and Reset remain.
Attach to left palm opens a mixed immersive scene with a native RealityKit left aboveHand
anchor and a small local offset. No skeleton data is read or stored; no custom hand recognizer
or raw hand-tracking permission is used. System hand menus remain available.
Float toolbar is available in the expanded dock and separately below the sculpture.

Limits and manual checks
Native AnchorEntity previews did not resolve visibly in this simulator session. The simulator
therefore uses an explicitly labeled stationary Entity preview. Hardware uses the real left-hand
aboveHand anchor; it does not use a head-locked or stationary fallback.
No physical Vision Pro is currently connected. Hand following, comfort, tracking loss/recovery,
physical eye/pinch delivery, and possible overlap with the Home indicator still require the headset.
Pairing, Developer Mode, device registration, and provisioning remain manual steps.
Automated targeting of the sculpture itself previously failed with an invalid accessibility
activation transform. State tests verify the gesture operations, not physical delivery.
